Project

One project, two deliverables. On Fridays you build an AI research tool on the Parley API. On Wednesdays you write the paper about it, at CHI Late-Breaking Work length. The tool is demoed live on the last day; the paper could be submitted to CHI in January.

Two ways to choose an idea

Track 1: join one of ours

The teaching team releases scoped ideas in week 3. Rank up to three by October 1. Projects are sized to fit the term and come with a mentor from the research team.

Track 2: propose your own

Pick a seed below or bring one of the five ideas you posted in week 3. Own proposals pass a feasibility gate before October 9. The gate tests feasibility, not interest; the characteristic failure is scope.

Feasibility gate

One page, for own proposals. Returned within a week with approve, revise once, or pick a seed. No penalty for picking a seed.

  1. Claim in one sentence, the one your evaluation could fail to support
  2. Genre and what counts as evidence in it
  3. Evidence you will have by the midpoint, named: one figure, one artifact, one table
  4. The minimum paper if everything after the midpoint fails
  5. What you cut if behind
  6. Access today: data on disk, equipment, participants. "I will get" counts as no
  7. Hours arithmetic: hours per week times weeks left, against your build-plus-write estimate
  8. AI plan: which parts AI does most of, and how you verify each

Red-flag words: longitudinal, deploy, recruit N, train a model.

Paper format

  • CHI Late-Breaking Work template and page limit, current call. The limit is the point
  • Includes: the paper, the AI log, and a one-page reflection: what you would cut for a real submission, what you would add with a year
  • Graded on: one claim the evidence could have failed to support; every section holds up an introduction sentence; related work says what prior work cannot do; a figure the finding can be read from; honest limitations and log
  • Not graded: whether the result is positive. A clean negative result with a tight argument is an excellent LBW

Tool requirements

  • Built on the Parley API with your class key; the key never enters the repo or the client
  • Skill plus script or above: a script that controls model, prompt, and token budget, not only a SKILL.md
  • An evaluation harness against a small ground-truth set; the report's first line is the tool's measured failure rate
  • Cost logged per run, compared with your pre-run estimate
  • Example projects: Reactant, AutoSurveyGPT
