Course readings: the hook papers

Seventeen award papers, one per fabrication subarea. Each is a door into a field: you will map what came before it, who disagrees with it, what happened after, and why it got attention. All are loaded as sample seeds in the related-work explorer. Your own seed is welcome; propose it by the end of week 2.
