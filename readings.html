Hook paper menu

Released in week 1. Ten to twelve papers, five to fifteen years old, mixed fields, at most two per student. Each paper was chosen to teach a different kind of lesson:

SlotWhat a good speedrun discovers
partly overturnedthe afterlife narrows or reverses the headline claim
methodologically weak but famousattention and rigor came apart; why it got cited anyway
solid but poorly writtenthe contribution is real and hard to find
live controversythe field has not settled; factions visible in the graph
solid, public dataa clean lineage and a figure you can reproduce

You will not be told which paper is in which slot. Opt-out for students with a thesis topic: proposal due end of week 2.

Strong LBWs we reverse-outline

Two to three short papers across genres (fabrication or system, study, theory). Posted before week 6.
