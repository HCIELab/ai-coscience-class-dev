What this is for

Reviews are predictable more often than authors expect. A fabrication reviewer asks about tolerances; a methods reviewer asks about power; a design researcher asks who this is for. Running the personas you are likely to draw, before you submit, tells you which section is under-defended. It is also the "Claude predicts the reviewers" half of the week 10 activity.

Two rules. The model is not a reviewer; it is a rehearsal partner, and it is confidently wrong sometimes, so check every quoted claim against your text. And a review it writes about someone else's confidential draft is a breach; only run it on your own work or on published papers.
