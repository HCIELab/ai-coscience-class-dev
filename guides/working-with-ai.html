Guide

Working with AI in this course

Use it fully. Log everything. Verify what you keep. Do the first pass by hand.

The AI log

Every lab, assignment, and the project include a log. One row per substantive exchange.

#What I askedWhat it claimedVerified? (source opened)ResultWhat I did with it
1who disagrees with the hook papernamed three papersopened all threeone correct, one misattributed, one does not existkept one, dropped two, logged the fake

End with the summary line: kept N · verified correct N · wrong and dropped N · unverifiable and dropped N.

The log is graded for honesty, not cleanliness. A log that shows the model was confidently wrong and you caught it scores higher than a clean page with no log.

Verification

  • Every claim about the literature that you keep, you verified against the source itself, not a summary of it
  • A DOI that resolves proves existence and nothing more. Alignment (does the source say that, at that strength) requires opening it
  • Could not verify means drop and flag, never keep
  • Do the first reverse outline and the first reading receipt by hand. Then ask the model and compare

Disclosure

  • Follow the ACM policy on generative AI: disclose use in the paper; you are responsible for every word and citation
  • Your final paper's AI log states which parts had AI involvement and at what level: idea, structure, prose, code, figures
  • Review packets used in class are confidential to the class. Do not paste them into any external tool

Cost and model habits

  • You have $30 a month of Parley API credit and a separate chat quota. Interactive calls are effectively free; agent loops on Opus can burn the month in one session
  • Default to Haiku or Llama for bulk, Sonnet on request, Opus never by default. Use plan mode before executing
  • Check spend on the Parley dashboard or the budget counter before and after any agentic session
  • Convert PDFs and slides to markdown before feeding them to a model; it is far cheaper in tokens
  • Keep a global CLAUDE.md with your writing rules (no em-dashes, no AI-sounding patterns) so every session inherits them
  • Keys live in your shell environment or ~/.claude/settings.json, never in a repo or a shared file
