Schedule

6.8500 is a graduate course on integrating large language models into the stages of HCI research. Wednesdays open with a lecture on what good and bad look like in research, followed by a hands-on activity with a casual checkoff. Fridays open with a five-minute introduction to the day's concept, such as what a harness or a skill is, followed by a long lab with a proper checkoff. Teams form by October 9, and both days feed one project.
