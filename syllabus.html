About

This course provides a practical overview of how artificial intelligence, particularly large language models, can be integrated across the stages of HCI research. You will learn to use AI tools for literature reviews, research question development, prototype design and implementation, technical and user evaluation, and paper writing. The course emphasizes methodological transparency, ethical usage, and reproducibility. It is designed for graduate students who want to enhance their research workflows without compromising academic rigor.

  • Critically evaluate and integrate AI into qualitative and quantitative research workflows
  • Use AI to develop research designs, construct theory, and synthesize literature
  • Recognize ethical concerns and apply responsible AI use practices

Policies

  • Late work. Labs: automatic one-week extension. Problem sets: escalating penalty. Project milestones: no extensions to confirm
  • Collaboration. Except for the project, labs and assignments are individual. You personally write the code, run the audits, and write the paragraphs
  • AI use. Allowed everywhere, logged everywhere, verified before kept. The full rules are in Working with AI in this course. The log is graded
  • Disclosure. Final papers follow the ACM generative-AI policy and include the AI log
  • Review packets. Real reviews used in class have reviewer identifiers removed and are confidential to the class. Do not redistribute or paste into external tools
  • Data. No student data (rosters, grades, submissions) lives on this site or in its repository
  • Cost. $30 a month of Parley API credit per student, separate from the chat quota. Haiku default; plan mode before executing; check spend before and after agentic sessions
