Lab N: title of the lab

One sentence on what this lab produces and which later lab reuses it.

Before you start

  • Paper to have read (link), and the pass you should have done on it
  • Tool this lab uses (link to the HTML lab)
  • File to bring from the previous lab

Explore 15 min

Two or three open prompts. No artifact yet.

  1. Prompt one
  2. Prompt two

Do 30 to 45 min

Numbered steps that produce one artifact in the tool.

  1. Step
  2. Step
  3. Step, export the artifact

Checkoff in lab

Shape test (a TA checks the export):

  • Structural minimum, for example every quadrant non-empty
  • Every source has a DOI or URL that resolves
  • AI log rows for this lab, with the kept / wrong / unverifiable line

Oral (two minutes, the TA picks one): "Point at the contribution sentence." "Which of these did you actually open? Show me."

Write 10 min

The paragraph this lab becomes in your paper. 150 words. Swap with a neighbor; mark the sentence where you got lost.

Deliverables

At the end of the lab, upload to your student folder:

  1. the artifact export (labN-artifact.json or .md)
  2. one screenshot of the finished view
  3. the Write paragraph (labN-paragraph.md)
  4. your AI log rows for this lab

Labs get an automatic one-week extension. Project milestones do not.
