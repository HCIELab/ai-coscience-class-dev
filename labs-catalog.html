Labs catalog · proposal

Labs students run in the browser

Thirteen browser labs for the research and writing track. One HTML page each, 20 to 45 minutes in class.

reading a field paper structure reviewing project build: S afternoon · M weekend · L week

Reading a field

session Abuild M

Field map canvas

Drag paper cards into before, disagrees, after, why. Empty quadrants show at a glance.

session Bbuild M

Graph overlay

Circle clusters on the citation graph. Mark what you read, what you missed, what Claude picked.

assignment 3build M

Citation drift auditor

Source passage beside each citing sentence. Three lights: exists, metadata, aligns.

assignment 2build S

Figure compare

Original beside your reproduction. Every discrepancy gets a hypothesis.

Paper structure

session Cbuild S

Scramble and reassemble

Drag shuffled paragraphs back into order. Name the signal behind each move.

session Cbuild M

Reverse outline builder

One sentence per paragraph, then arrows. Orphans and the contribution sentence surface.

session Cbuild S

Section ablation

Switch a section off. See which introduction sentence loses its support.

session Cbuild S

Paired A/B reader

Two versions of one introduction. Click the sentence where you decided.

session Dbuild M

Empty LBW tagger

Tag every sentence of a Claude-written paper about nothing: evidence, claim, filler, generic.

session Dbuild M

Genre skeleton sketcher

Stack section boxes by page share for your genre. Overlay the room's drawings.

Reviewing

session Ebuild M

Review prediction sheet

Predict what a real rejected draft's authors accepted, rebutted, ignored. Then v2 unlocks.

Project and the shared log

session Fbuild M

Seed picker and feasibility gate

Filter seeds by method. The gate does the hours arithmetic and flags scope words.

every labbuild S

AI log

Asked, claimed, verified, result, did with it. One format, every lab.
