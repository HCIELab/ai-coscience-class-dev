Interactive Activities

On Wednesdays, you will use these tools to explore our lecture materials such as related work exploration and argument forming. The tool will visualize your thought process, where you can either use the interactive tool we have, or upload your chat history with your agentic tool for TAs to check off. During the check off, the TA will ask you to explain your thought process and your graph.

weeks 3, 5, 9, projectbeta

Related-work explorer

Import a bibliography (your search, or BibTeX from a Claude chat) or a seed DOI. Every entry resolves against OpenAlex in your browser; unresolved ones are flagged red. With your own Parley key, ask the model for its top papers and see where they land. Open the explorer.

weeks 6, 11, 13beta

Paper outline

Upload a PDF or paste your draft. One sentence per paragraph on what it does for the argument, dependency arrows, what each section holds up, and the checks. Write yours first, then reveal the model's. Open the outline tool.

weeks 8, 10planned

Empty LBW tagger

Tag every sentence as evidence, claim without evidence, filler, or generic. Ratio bar, checklist. Works on Claude's paper about nothing and on your own draft.

weeks 9, 13beta

Reviewer predictor

Pick the reviewers your paper might draw (fabrication, methods, design, AI, and more), edit what each cares about, get independent reviews and a meta-review. Write your own prediction first. Open the reviewer predictor.

every weekplanned

AI log

Asked, claimed, verified, result, did with it. One row per exchange, one format for every lab and the project. Exports to markdown.
